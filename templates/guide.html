WASTE SEGREGATION

Waste segregation guide

🍌 Organic

Fruit peels, vegetable scraps and food leftovers. Use for composting.

🧴 Plastic

Clean bottles and containers. Send for recycling where accepted.

📄 Paper

Newspapers and cardboard. Keep dry for recycling.

🍾 Glass

Glass bottles and jars. Handle carefully.

🥫 Metal

Clean cans and metal containers. Keep separate.

🔋 E-waste

Batteries and electronics. Use an authorised collection centre.
